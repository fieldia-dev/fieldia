What Azure DevOps teaches our forms

I went through your twelve screenshots of Azure DevOps Boards: the Kanban board, the backlog, and a bug's work item with each control open (state, area, iteration, rich text, comment, links, history). This page lists what Fieldia already covers, what it covers in part, what it lacks, and what I would add, in the order I would build it.

Screens: board, backlog list, work item form (read state, editing State, Area and Iteration pickers, Repro Steps in Markdown, System Info in rich text, a new comment, the Add link menu), and the History tab. No screenshot is copied into this page: every sketch below is drawn the way a Fieldia form would draw it.

The layout: mostly covered

Three columns of titled groups, labels above or beside, compact density and collapsible groups are all already in Fieldia's page format.

The controls: the real gap

DevOps fields read as plain text until you point at them. Ours always look like boxes. A quiet look is the single change that would make Fieldia feel like this.

Missing parts

A path picker with suggestions, a record header bar with tabs that carry counts, and a state history with before and after. The board comes from Grafloria.

What I would add

Six additions, each sketched as a Fieldia page would draw it. Sizes are rough: S is days, M about a week.

1. A quiet look for fields

Biggest gapSize M

Seen in: the work item header and Planning group. Values read as text; the box only appears when you point at it or click.

  • A third skin beside underline and outlined: skin: "quiet".
  • At rest, a value is text with no box. Pointing shows a faint box; focus shows the full input.
  • An empty field says what to do, in the muted colour ("Add a reason").
  • Selections keep their coloured dot at rest and in the open list, with a tick on the chosen option.
  • A field locked by a rule shows a lock; pointing at it or focusing it says why ("Set when the bug is resolved").
  • Keyboard first: Tab lands on each field with a visible ring. Nothing is hidden behind hover.

2. A record header bar

Extends the sheet headerSize M

Seen in: the top of every work item.

  • A kind: a badge and a colour strip at the start edge, set by a field (a bug red, a story blue).
  • The ID beside the title, which is edited in place.
  • A row for the assignee, comment count and tags.
  • "Updated by Nour Kamal, 25 Aug" from the record's own fields.
  • Tabs at the end with an icon and a count: History, Links 0, Attachments 1.
  • Save as a split button: Save, and its menu with Save and close.
  • Follow, and Undo all changes.

DevOps greys out Save and Close until something changes. We would show Save always, and say "Saved" when there is nothing new, as our forms already do.

3. A path picker with suggestions

Extends the link fieldSize M

Seen in: Area and Iteration. Recently used paths first, then the full tree; each iteration's dates right-aligned.

  • A link field to a record with a parent (parentField) shows the whole path, "Portal\Sprint 14".
  • The list opens with Suggestions (recent and frequent for this person), then the tree, folded to the current branch.
  • A second column of the record's own field, right-aligned (dates, a code, a balance).
  • Typing filters the tree and keeps each match's parents shown.
  • The arrow keys move through the tree; Right opens a branch, Left folds it.

4. Rich text that stays out of the way

Extends formatted textSize M

Seen in: System Info and the comment box. The toolbar appears only while the field has the focus.

  • At rest, formatted text is shown as text; empty, it says what goes there ("Add system info").
  • The toolbar appears on focus and goes on blur, as in the quiet look.
  • @ for a person and # for a record, from the data source's search, kept as links in the text.
  • Inline code, strike-through, highlight, and an image pasted or dropped in (stored through the app's files).
  • A Markdown mode with Preview for fields that keep Markdown, set by the field.

6. A filter bar, one dropdown per field

Another face of the searchSize S

Seen in: the backlog. Keyword on the left; Work item type, State, Area, Iteration and Tags as dropdowns of checkboxes.

  • A list option, "search": { "style": "bar" }, the same filter underneath, so favourites and the map keep working.
  • Each named field is a dropdown with a checkbox per value and its count.
  • A field with something picked shows it ("Active") in the accent; Clear all at the end.

What not to copy

Buttons greyed until something changes

Save and Close is grey and does nothing until you edit. Our rule is to hide or explain, never disable without a word: Save shows, and says "Saved" when there is nothing new.

Icon-only buttons

Refresh, revert, settings and the history tab are icons alone. Ours keep a name on screen or in a tooltip, and for screen readers.

Actions shown only on hover

A group's expand and collapse appear when the pointer is over it. Ours appear on focus too, so keyboard and touch get them.

Locks with no reason

Reason and Resolved Reason show a lock and nothing more. A lock should say why, and when it will open.
