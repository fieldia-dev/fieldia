Codes to scan

Open this page on another screen, or print it, and hold a code up to the camera when a demo asks you to scan. The codes are the demos’ own: a patient’s card, an injectable’s box, a shelf’s products.

Patients’ cards (QR codes)

For the treatment demo: press Scan beside Patient.

Injectables’ boxes (GS1 DataMatrix)

For the treatment demo: press Scan beside a line’s Lot. The product, the lot and the expiry come from the one code.

A shelf’s products (EAN-13 barcodes)

For the stock count demo: press Scan under the table and read each in turn — every read counts one.
